cellmap_flow.dashboard.routes.finetune.restart_key

Shift+R over the viewer: restart the finetune job the Finetune tab shows, with the tab’s settings.

It does what the Training Status card’s Restart button does, without the button’s confirm (pressing the key is a deliberate act), from the viewer, where the user paints. The form’s values are in the page, not here, so the key does not restart the job itself. Its action checks the job and counts the press against it; the job’s status answer, which the Finetune tab polls every 3 s while it follows a job (job-monitor.js’s startStatusPolling), carries the count as restart_requests; and the tab, seeing the count go up, sends the restart as Restart does. So the key acts within one poll, up to 3 s, and the restart route then syncs MinIO as for the button.

The job is the one the tab follows, the last whose status it asked for, which is the one its card shows. The key counts a press only for a job that can take a restart (state.can_restart, which is when the card offers Restart), and otherwise says why not, in neuroglancer’s status bar, as ai_annotate’s Shift+G does: there is no job, or it is still running, or it has finished. A press while an earlier one waits for its restart is ignored, saying so, as the button is disabled while its request runs.

The status bar says what will happen at the press; then what the restart route answered (taken as it starts, answered once it has), or, when no restart came within PENDING_S (the dashboard’s page is closed, or it refused, saying why in the tab), that none did. Each of those goes after SHOW_S.

Shift+R pressed with the focus in the dashboard’s page, and the browser mode’s key (web/src/backend/routes/finetune.ts binds it in the page), go straight to job-monitor.js, which says why not in the tab itself.

Bind Shift+R to the restart action in the session’s viewer, once per viewer.

The routes that call this are the job list, which the Finetune tab asks for on every page load (and connect.js reloads the page after a new dataset), and a job’s status, which it polls while it follows a job.
