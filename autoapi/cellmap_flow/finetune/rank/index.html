cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.empty_tallies(channels: int) → List[dict]

One channel’s agreement with the paint per channel: the targets counted, their logits binned against CUTOFFS (bin b: above b of them), and the loss summed, apart for object and background targets.

cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.paint_edges(labels: numpy.ndarray) → numpy.ndarray

The painted voxels of labels (z, y, x; 0 unpainted, 1 background, 2 and up objects) that touch a painted voxel of the other class along z, y or x: the strokes’ edges, which a binary model’s score leaves out.

cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.rank_score(tallies: List[dict], channels: Sequence[str], affinity: bool) → dict | None

The score of tallies: an affinity model’s over its channels together, a binary model’s best channel’s (by the score, then the lower loss). None when nothing was counted.

cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.spread(items: Sequence, count: int) → list

count of items, spread evenly over them in their order; all of them when there are no more.

cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.tally(tallies: List[dict], target: numpy.ndarray, mask: numpy.ndarray, logits: numpy.ndarray, skip: numpy.ndarray | None = None) → None

Add one painted block to tallies: target and mask (C, z, y, x) as the finetune’s target transform makes them, logits the model’s on the same voxels (NaN where it was not read), and skip (z, y, x) the voxels not to count (paint_edges), or None.

cellmap_flow.finetune.rank.windowed(norm: Sequence[dict], lo: float, hi: float) → List[dict] | None

norm (normalizer specs, {"name": ..., ...}) with its MinMaxNormalizer set to the raw’s window [lo, hi], or None: no MinMaxNormalizer, no window (lo = hi), or one not narrower than NARROW_WINDOW of the normalizer’s own range.
