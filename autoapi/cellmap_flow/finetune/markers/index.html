cellmap_flow.finetune.markers

The lines the trainer prints for the job manager to find in its log.

The training job’s loop (session_loop) and lora_trainer print these to stdout, which tee copies into training_log.txt; the job manager reads them back from there to follow the job, and the finetune tab reads the log it streams. The two sides can be different versions – a training job outlives a dashboard upgrade – so these only ever change compatibly.

The patterns are the ones the job manager has always used, unchanged.
