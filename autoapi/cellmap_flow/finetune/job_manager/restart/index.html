cellmap_flow.finetune.job_manager.restart

Asking a finetune job that waits for a restart to train again.

The trainer’s inference server, in the same process as its training loop, takes POST <server>/__control__/restart with the job’s restart token (written into the run’s directory at submit; serving.restart_token) in the X-Restart-Token header. The body is {"restart": true, "timestamp", "params"}, params being the settings to change. When the server cannot be reached, or has no URL yet, the same body goes into the run’s restart_signal.json, which the trainer also watches (finetune.session_loop). It is written whole or not at all (finetune.json_files.write_json_atomically): the trainer reads it as soon as it exists, and ends the job if it is not JSON. Jobs outlive dashboard upgrades, so both ways are a protocol.

Ask job’s trainer to train again, with updated_params changed.

The job is then RUNNING a new iteration from epoch 0, its server not ready until the iteration completes, and job.params updated. Raises ValueError, and sends nothing, unless the job can take a restart (state.can_restart).
