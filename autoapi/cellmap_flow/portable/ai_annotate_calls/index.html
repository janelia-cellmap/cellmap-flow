cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls

What the browser calls for an AI annotation: the functions web/src/backend/ai-annotate-job.ts and web/src/ai-annotate/gemini.ts run through chunkmirage’s Engine.call("cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls:<name>", args, arrays) (chunkmirage.calls says the shape: fn(args, arrays), args the JSON arguments, arrays the bytes sent beside them, in order; a JSON value, bytes, or a pair of JSON and arrays back). Each decodes its arrays, calls the maths in ai_annotate.py and fill.py and encodes the answer; the maths is not here. Images travel as the canvas holds them, RGBA bytes rows first; label boxes in the volume’s dtype, C order (z, y, x).

A refusal meant for the user (a point outside the volume or the data, ids running out) is answered as {"refused": message} rather than raised: the worker would hand the page Python’s traceback with it, where the page shows the message alone, as the server’s routes do. Nothing here logs or keeps anything: an image’s bytes go no further than the answer.

Module Contents

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.plan(args, arrays)

The plane through the point (ai_annotate.plan_plane) and the raw it reads (raw_box). args: volume (its output_voxel_size, input_voxel_size and dataset_offset_nm), volume_shape, point_nm, depth_axis, crop_size_px, data_box_nm and display_dimensions (each null for none), and raw, the grid of the raw level read (voxel, and corner, its voxel 0’s lower corner in nm). Answers the plan (PlanePlan.to_dict), its click_px and write_shape, and the raw_box (begin, count); or the refusal.

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.request_image(args, arrays)

The image sent for plan’s plane: the raw block its raw_box read (arrays[0], count voxels z, y, x of dtype, 0 past the data) laid out and resampled (ai_annotate.plane_image) and scaled to 8-bit grey (normalize_to_uint8), as the server’s read_plane and build_request make it. Answers its RGBA bytes, the plan’s image_shape, the grey in every channel.

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.model_mask(args, arrays)

The mask in a model’s reply, and the reply lined up with the plane (ai_annotate.model_mask). args: shape (rows, cols) of the reply as decoded, sent and image (rows, cols of the image sent, padded square, and of the plane), target_rgb; arrays: the reply’s RGBA bytes. Answers {} with the mask (a byte a pixel of the plane, 1 where painted) and the lined-up reply (RGBA).

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.prepare(args, arrays)

Nothing, once scipy.ndimage is imported, which stage labels the mask’s objects with: the browser calls this while the model works, so scipy’s first load (seconds, once a page) is over by the time the answer comes.

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.stage(args, arrays)

What the review shows of a mask, as the server stages it: its objects on the write box (ai_annotate.mask_to_write_shape), the share of the box they mark, and the image sent with them tinted (overlay). args: plan and target_rgb; arrays: the mask (a byte a pixel of the plan’s image, nonzero where painted) and the image sent (RGBA). Answers mask_fraction with the objects (int32, the write box’s in-plane shape, z, y, x order) and the overlay (RGBA).

cellmap_flow.portable.ai_annotate_calls.accept(args, arrays)

What Accept writes into the write box: ai_annotate.accept_labels, written over the box as fill.paint writes. args: depth_axis, shape (the write box’s, z, y, x), dtype (the volume’s), instance_target (the model is trained on instances), overwrite, and beside: whether the planes before and after the box along the depth axis are sent (false past the volume’s edge). arrays: the objects (int32, as stage answered them), the box as the volume holds it now, then the planes beside that are sent, each shaped as the box. Answers the counts (n_foreground, n_background, n_overwritten) with the box to write, or no array when nothing changes; or the refusal, when the ids run out.
