cellmap_flow.portable.carry

The paint of one annotation volume carried onto another model’s grid: the maths of carry_paint (finetune/session/volume.py says what it is for), on arrays alone, so the server runs it on its zarrs and the browser on its paint store’s chunks (portable/calls.py).

Each new voxel takes the paint of the old voxels whose centres lie inside it (one for a grid as fine as the old, several for a coarser one), or, where none does (a finer grid), the old voxel its own centre lies in. A new voxel whose old voxels were painted different labels is left unpainted, on a boundary the coarser grid cannot draw; unpainted old voxels do not count against it, so a stroke thinner than the new voxel still lands, a voxel wide. Only the boxes of the old volume’s painted chunks are visited.

cellmap_flow.portable.carry.carry(old: Grid, new: Grid, painted: Iterable[Tuple[tuple, numpy.ndarray]], read: Callable[[list, list], numpy.ndarray], dtype) → Tuple[Dict[tuple, numpy.ndarray], dict]

Carry the paint of the old volume’s painted chunks (each its index and its labels, a whole chunk) onto the new grid. read(lo, hi) gives the old labels over a box in old voxels (several chunks’ worth, for a coarser new grid); dtype is the new volume’s (its labels capped at its maximum).

Returns the new chunks that got paint, by index, each whole (0 where the old paint says nothing: the caller writes them over what is there where they are not 0), and the counts {"painted", "source", "conflicts", "chunks"}: the new voxels painted, the old painted voxels they came from, the new voxels left unpainted where their old voxels disagreed, and the new chunks returned.
