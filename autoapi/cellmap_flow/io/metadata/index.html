cellmap_flow.io.metadata

Array metadata for every format cellmap-flow reads, as one ArrayMeta.

worldarray parses the metadata: zarr v2 and v3, N5 and neuroglancer precomputed, on disk or at an http(s), s3 or gs URL (worldarray.placement, its spec’s Conventions 1 and 4). This module turns worldarray’s record (the store’s own units, translation the centre of voxel 0) into cellmap-flow’s: every axis kept, in the array’s own (C) order; spatial sizes and translations in nanometer floats; translation the lower corner of voxel 0; non-spatial axes with unit None, voxel size 1 and translation 0. .spatial() drops the channel/time axes.

What stays here because worldarray has no such thing:

  • ArrayMeta itself (corner-based, spatial() a method);

  • the record’s shape for precomputed (a leading channel axis, even of one channel) and for unnamed axes (c^ before z, y, x);

  • precomputed levels named s<N> (worldarray names them by key; it opens …/s<N> as scale N);

  • list_levels refusing a group without multiscales;

  • nm_per_unit, over worldarray’s unit table (worldarray.units.TABLE);

  • the crop loader’s attribute helpers (read_zarr_json, attrs_from_meta, legacy_attrs, dataset_transforms).

class cellmap_flow.io.metadata.ArrayMeta

What one array is, independent of its format.

Every per-axis tuple has one entry per array dimension, in the array’s (C) order. units[i] is “nanometer” on a spatial axis – its voxel_size and translation have been converted to nm – and None on a channel or time axis, where voxel_size is 1.0 and translation 0.0. translation is the lower corner of voxel 0 (worldarray’s corner; its translation is the centre). An axis nobody named has the name “”.

format is “zarr2”, “zarr3”, “n5” or “precomputed”.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.from_world_array(native: worldarray.ArrayMeta, path: str) → ArrayMeta

cellmap-flow’s record of worldarray’s native metadata (an array’s .meta) of the array at path.

worldarray’s spatial axes are those named z, y or x, else the last three; the others keep unit None. Unnamed axes get cellmap-flow’s names (z, y, x from the end, c^ before them); a precomputed volume’s channel axis, which worldarray drops when there is one channel, is put back.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.open_level(path: str, voxel_size=None, **kwargs) → worldarray.WorldArray

The worldarray array path names, as cellmap-flow reads a path: an array as it is, …/s<N> of a precomputed volume as scale N, and a group or precomputed volume at its level for voxel_size (worldarray chooses it from the group’s document), its first without one.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.read_array_meta(path: str) → ArrayMeta

Metadata of the array at path (see the module docstring). A path at a group reads its first level: the first its multiscales list, else s0.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.level_names(group_path: str, pyramid: worldarray.Pyramid) → List[str]

cellmap-flow’s names of pyramid’s levels: a precomputed volume’s scales are s<N>, a group’s levels their dataset paths.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.pyramid(group_path: str, units=None) → Tuple[str, worldarray.Pyramid]

(group path, worldarray Pyramid) of the multiscale group or precomputed volume at group_path, in units (native when None); raises as list_levels does.

cellmap_flow.io.metadata.list_levels(group_path: str) → List[Tuple[str, ArrayMeta]]

[(dataset path, ArrayMeta)] for each level of the OME multiscale group at group_path, in the order its datasets list them, or of the precomputed volume there (s<N> for scale N), from the documents alone (worldarray.Pyramid.metas): no level is opened.

Raises when there is no such group (KeyError for a v2 group without multiscales, ValueError for a v3 one, or for one scale of a precomputed volume).
