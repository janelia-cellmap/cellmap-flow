cellmap_flow.dashboard.services
===============================

.. py:module:: cellmap_flow.dashboard.services

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   What the dashboard's routes do beyond answering a request.

   - ``launch``: start the models picked on the Models tab, show each in the
     viewer, and stop the ones unpicked.
   - ``startup``: the CLIs' last step, the viewer with their models, then the
     dashboard itself.
